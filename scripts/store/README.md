# Store assets

`build_play_assets.py` turns raw 1080×2400 release-build captures into the designed Google Play phone screenshots
(1080×1920) and the 1024×500 feature graphic, English and Urdu. Two styles: `pro` (default, copy in
`captions_pro.json`) and `classic` (`--style classic`, copy in `captions.json`). Usage, capture list and design notes:
`docs/play-console/STORE_LISTING_v2.md` → "Screenshots and feature graphic (T5)".
